*Rules for the*

*determination*

30.8 The **expert** is:

(a) to make a decision according to justice and fairness;

(b) to conduct the **dispute** reference speedily, without formality and in

such manner as the **expert** considers suitable (including proceeding

ex parte after due notice);

(c) able to:

(i) inspect or copy any document of the parties;

(ii) take any sample or make any test;

(iii) require any party to attend any meeting and appoint a time and

place for it;

(iv) engage and consult with any advisers, legal or technical, after

prior notice to both parties of the advisers and the matters being

consulted on;

(v) require any moneys in **dispute** and moneys towards the costs of

the **dispute** reference to be paid into a trust account; and

(vi) direct how such moneys held in trust are to be paid;

(d) to give a decision in writing stating the issues in **dispute** and the

**expert's** determination of each issue;

(e) to give such decision within 14 days of the referral of the **dispute**;

and

(f) not required to give reasons for the decision.

*Cooperation*

30.9 The parties must cooperate with the **expert**.

*Documents used*

*All information and*

*documents*

*exchanged is "in*

*confidence".*

30.10 Any information or documents obtained through or as part of the **dispute**

reference must not be used for any other purpose.

*Decision is final*

30.11 Except as to questions of law, the **expert's** decision is final and binding on

the parties and neither party may challenge the decision in any other

proceedings.

*Debt due*

30.12 Where the **expert's** decision includes an order for the payment of moneys,

such amount is immediately due and payable.

*No prejudice*

30.13 Nothing herein prejudices the right of a party to institute proceedings to

enforce payment due under the **contract** or to seek injunctive or

declaratory relief.

*Preservation of rights*

30.14 Notwithstanding the existence of a **dispute**, the parties must, subject to

Clause 17 and the preceding paragraph of this Clause 30, continue to

perform the **contract**.
